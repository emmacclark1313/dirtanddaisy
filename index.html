START YOUR GARDEN TODAY

Starting a garden at home is easier than you think! We’re here to help you discover what to plant—and when—based on your climate and location. Gardening isn’t just about the produce—it’s about connecting with nature, soaking up sunshine, and getting your hands dirty. No matter your skill level, you can grow something beautiful. Whether it’s flowers, veggies, or herbs, your garden is waiting to become your new favorite place.

My Gardening Journey

For a long time, I put off starting a garden because I was convinced I wouldn’t be able to grow anything. I thought I didn’t have the right skills, the right tools, or even the right yard. But the summer I finally gave it a try, I ended up with more veggies than I knew what to do with! I was giving them away to friends and family, and even pickling the extras. Gardening quickly became one of the most rewarding things I’ve ever done — and it can be for you too.
